# 15 Years of Code Baking!

Today marks a monumental milestone in our journey – **15 years** of Srapsware! What started as a small team with big dreams has grown into a thriving software development company serving clients worldwide.

## A Journey Through Time

### 2011: The Beginning

It all started with a simple vision: to create exceptional software solutions that make a real difference. From our humble beginnings, we've grown from a small startup to a trusted technology partner for businesses across the globe.

### 2011-2016: Building Foundations

During our first five years, we focused on:
- Establishing our core values of quality, innovation, and client satisfaction
- Building a talented team of developers, designers, and project managers
- Delivering our first major projects in e-commerce and web development
- Creating lasting relationships with our initial clients who trusted us with their vision

### 2016-2021: Expansion & Growth

The next phase saw us:
- Expanding our service offerings to include mobile app development, cloud solutions, and DevOps
- Growing our team to 50+ talented professionals
- Opening new opportunities in international markets
- Adopting cutting-edge technologies like React, Node.js, and cloud-native architectures
- Successfully delivering 500+ projects across various industries

### 2021-2026: Innovation & Excellence

The last five years have been transformative:
- Embracing modern frameworks like Next.js, React Native, and serverless architectures
- Launching AI/ML-powered solutions for our clients
- Achieving a 98% client satisfaction rate
- Building long-term partnerships with Fortune 500 companies
- Contributing to open-source projects and giving back to the developer community

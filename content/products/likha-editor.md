## Built for the writing experience

Likha Editor brings a configurable toolbar and rich text editing into web applications. The project is pre-1.0; consult its release status and documentation before choosing capabilities for your product.

Explore the maintained examples, integration guides and repository on the official product site. If your application needs custom editing workflows, we can discuss how to integrate and adapt the experience.

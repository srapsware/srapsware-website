WhoisExtractor MCP exposes product workflows to compatible AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol. Its public connection guide describes account-based authorization, permission scopes and protected write operations.

### From a request to a product workflow

The published integration covers account details, orders, available downloads, invoices, product information and support tickets. Users connect their existing WhoisExtractor account through the supported authorization flow.

### A focused integration example

The public service describes OAuth 2.1 and scoped access. Client support and setup depend on the client’s available MCP capabilities; the maintained connection instructions live on the project website.
